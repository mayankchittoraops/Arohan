# Arohan — Exercise Reference

Every movement the app can prescribe, with a YouTube search link for each so you can
watch the form once and build a playlist. Generated from the app itself by
`scripts/exercise-reference.mts`, so it cannot drift from what Arohan actually asks of you.

**83 movements** · **16 workout templates** across 4 phases ·
**8 mobility routines**

> The search links are searches, not specific videos — a link to one channel rots, a
> search does not. Pick a video where the coach explains the setup and shows a side angle.

---

## How to use this

Do not try to watch all 83. Phase 1 is the only thing you will touch for three months, and
**[Start here](#start-here)** lists exactly what it uses — ordered by how often each
movement actually comes up, so the top of that list is where the value is. The first dozen
cover most of what you will do in a given week.

The whole programme is built around a lower back that complains, so two things are worth
more attention than the rest:

- **The hip hinge.** Deadlifts, good mornings, hip hinges and kettlebell-style patterns all
  depend on it. Watching one good hinge tutorial pays for itself across a dozen movements.
- **Bracing, not crunching.** Planks, bird dogs, dead bugs and Pallof presses train the
  trunk to resist movement. If a video has you curling the spine under load, it is teaching
  something this programme deliberately avoids.

Every movement in the app carries an easier and a harder variation on its own screen. If a
video shows a version that feels wrong today, the easier one is one tap away.
